What happens to your data?

Deletion removes the account record, including email, name, profile image, password hash, linked wallets, and verification and reset tokens. Saved decks, inventory, party membership, and matchmaking tickets are removed. Identifying fields are removed from affected match records. Combined gameplay totals are retained without a player identifier.

Payment records retained for tax and audit

Account deletion does not erase payment and accounting records we are legally required to retain for taxation and auditing. We retain only the necessary records for the required period and remove unnecessary identifying information and links to your game account. These records cannot be used to recover your deleted account. Arbor Studios does not collect or store payment card numbers, card security codes, or bank account details; payments will be processed through Google Play.

Google may retain its own payment records under its policies and legal obligations. Any retained transaction records that can identify a person remain protected as personal data.

The actual payment-record fields and legal retention period, along with backup, hosting-log, and provider retention details, must be confirmed before this draft is published. Active database deletion does not itself erase separate backups or information held independently by Google. See our privacy policy.
