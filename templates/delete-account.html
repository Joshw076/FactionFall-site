What happens to your data?

Deletion removes the account record, including email, name, profile image, password hash, linked wallets, and verification and reset tokens. Saved decks, inventory, party membership, and matchmaking tickets are removed. Identifying fields are removed from affected match records. Combined gameplay totals are retained without a player identifier.

Deletion also removes linked Play Games identity, purchase-token hash and entitlement, Flux rewards and ad event records, ranked ratings and related account records. Match metadata, team and deck index, timestamps, and other participants' records can remain; these records are not assumed anonymous.

Payments and records held separately

The current backend removes your purchase-token hash with your account and has no separate accounting ledger. Financial reports retained outside the game database may need to be kept for taxation or auditing. Any actual retention period and required fields must be confirmed before publication. These records cannot restore a deleted game account. The purchase integration does not request or store payment card numbers, card security codes, or bank account details.

Google may retain its own payment records under its policies and legal obligations. Any retained transaction records that can identify a person remain protected as personal data.

Backup, hosting-log, and provider retention/deletion details must be confirmed before this draft is published. Active database deletion does not itself erase separate backups or information held by Google, Unity, or advertising providers. See our privacy policy.
